### me

I'm a computer engineer who works on the **systems** side of quantum computing, where hardware, software and the physics underneath have to fit together. Most of my track record is in **measurement and characterization**, from FPGA and acquisition work on physics experiments to analysing how a quantum device holds up over time, with code anyone can rerun and check. From there I'm interested on the whole stack :) into **quantum error correction** and the **transpilation** processes

- 🔸 **TU Delft (2026):** as a visiting student, my MSc thesis worked on how long a quantum computer stays as good as its benchmarks say, and how much of its time goes to calibration and benchmarking. The analysis tool created is [QUEBRA](https://github.com/seraconti/quebra).
- 🔸 **CERN, LHCb (2025):** summer student on the timing distribution test benches. I built [ACAI](https://github.com/seraconti/acai), data acquisition for their clock-stability tests, and co-authored the [RT2026 contribution](https://indico.global/event/14708/contributions/146606/) on this work.
- 🔸 **CEPOF, USP (2023–24):** digital control on an FPGA for an atomic fountain clock, and a lot of Allan deviation plots.

MSc at Politecnico di Milano, BSc at Universidade de São Paulo (double degree).

Huge fan and supporter of Free and Open Software, as well as open knowledge. I am certain that collaboration and transparency are the ways to make more science of more quality, and have more fun doing it :)

### featured projects

#### [QUEBRA](https://github.com/seraconti/quebra): QUantum Engineering Reliability Analysis
> Takes benchmarking and characterization data recorded over time and measures how long a system stays in spec and how long it takes to come back. Works with any metric and platform, and every figure traces back to the exact data and code that made it.

#### [ACAI](https://github.com/seraconti/acai): Automated Clock-Oriented Acquisition Infrastructure
> Data acquisition for the LHCb timing test benches: an event-driven stack that runs measurement jobs without ever blocking the acquisition loop.
